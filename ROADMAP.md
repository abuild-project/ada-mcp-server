# Development roadmap

The first standalone pilot prerelease is 0.2.0-rc.1. The
[release plan](doc/plans/release-0-2-0-rc-1.md) separates reviewed release
preparation from publication of the tested artifacts. Public artifact
availability, anonymous registry access and a fresh managed Cloud acquisition
test require direct evidence from that publication and its consumers.

Actual macOS and other native clients are subsequent acceptance topics. Other
fork fixes and upstream proposals are evaluated separately; they are not
silently included in this bootstrap/distribution change.
